Group-level framing

DORA gives you four hours to report. Most SOCs take seventy-two.

Beyond Cards, this is the structural problem across UBS's ICT estate: European regulators now demand continuous risk monitoring and rapid incident classification — evidence, not assurances. Three gaps determine whether that evidence exists when an examiner asks for it.

Gap 01 · Alert volume outruns capacity
Roughly 11,000 alerts per day across a Tier-1 bank's ICT estate, with the majority resolving as noise — the real signal buried in triage backlog.
Gap 02 · Isolation is policy, not proof
Client A's deal data and Client B's are separated by access control lists on shared infrastructure — not by anything that survives a credential compromise.
Gap 03 · Visibility stops at Tier 1
Core banking, custody, and payment dependencies extend several tiers into fintech and infrastructure vendors — where most disruption and breach sources originate.
"Folder permissions are not security. Mathematical isolation is."
