Strategic Positioning

mcxOS is built on N-Tier Multi-Tenant architecture with Row-Level Security policies and enterprise-grade RBAC (role → permission → role_permission many-to-many on SQLAlchemy ORM). It sits on top of existing operating systems with an Embrace & Replace approach.

System of Record

mcxOS becomes the single source of truth. All master data, transactions, and financial records live here. Legacy systems feed into mcxOS as the canonical data store.

SOR Mode

System of Engagement

SOE Mode
