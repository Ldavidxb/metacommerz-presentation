The Problem: Vendor Lock-In

Your encryption keys live in AWS KMS. Your HSM is manufactured by Thales (France) or Entrust (US). Your TLS terminates on Cloudflare. Each vendor is a jurisdictional exposure point — subject to CLOUD Act, foreign surveillance laws, and supply chain compromise. You cannot audit their code.

Your drug formulations, genomic datasets, and clinical trial results — encrypted with RSA-2048 or ECDH — will be readable. Patent applications filed today must remain confidential for 20+ years.
